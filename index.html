About the developer

Code meets
play.

I’m Luna Lloyd, a game developer with a BSc in Computer Science, specializing in IT and UX, with a BSc in Computer Science and an MSc in Game Development.

I bring together technical curiosity and a love of games to create interactive experiences. I’ve self-published three games so far: Warped, a Unity text-to-speech demo, AmazeBalls, a time-trial maze game, and Silvestris, a Kingdom-inspired strategy prototype.

More games are in development such as Dungeon Keeper a papers please and dungeon crawler carl inspired rougue-lite. Follow along on itch.io.
